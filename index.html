Questions

Good to know before you start.

How much does a website cost?

A simple one-page website is £280, paid once. It includes a mobile-friendly design, your photos, menu or services, opening hours, contact details and a map, a booking button if you use Fresha, Booksy or similar, and one round of changes. Need more pages or extras? Get in touch for a quote.

What does hosting & care include?

Hosting & care is £20 a month, or £200 a year (two months free). It covers your .co.uk domain name, hosting and security, free email forwarding such as hello@yourbusiness.co.uk, and small text and photo updates.

Who owns the domain name?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name, and it's included in the hosting & care plan.

Can customers book online through my website?

Yes. If you already use a booking system such as Fresha or Booksy, we add a booking button that takes customers straight to it.

Can I see a design before I commit?

Yes. We'll make a free mock-up of your homepage so you can see how your site could look before you decide.

Do you use website builders like Wix or Squarespace?

No. Every site is designed from scratch and hand-coded, so it loads quickly and looks like your business rather than a template.

Which areas do you cover?

We work with small businesses in Belper, Duffield, Ambergate and across Derbyshire.
